# Dynalist Inc

**What they do:** Make [[Obsidian]] — and the outliner Dynalist before it. Founded by Shida Li and Erica Xu.

## Key facts

- Obsidian is famously not venture-funded and has no cloud lock-in: the business model is optional paid Sync/Publish on top of local plain-text files. That's the reason this vault can be plain markdown in git.
- [[Steph Ango]] is CEO and the author of the official Obsidian skills vendored in `.claude/skills/`.
