# Voyage AI

**What they do:** Embedding and reranking models; now owned by MongoDB. [[Anthropic]] officially points to them for embeddings, since Anthropic ships none of its own.

## Key facts

- **Voyage 4 family** launched Jan 2026: `voyage-4-large`, `voyage-4`, `voyage-4-lite`, and `voyage-4-nano` (open-weight, Apache 2.0). All 32K context, 1024 dims by default (256/512/2048 options).
- Rough pricing: voyage-4-large ~$0.12/M tokens, voyage-4 ~$0.06/M, voyage-4-lite ~$0.02/M, nano free to self-host. A vault of a few thousand notes is ~1–5M tokens — effectively free either way.
- Also ship `voyage-context-4` (contextualized chunk embeddings) and `rerank-2.5`.

## Why it matters to me

The natural paid upgrade if local embeddings ever feel too weak — but at this vault's size, local models are free and good enough. Noted so the decision is already researched when it comes up.
