# Andrej Karpathy

**How we know of him:** Originator of the "LLM wiki" idea that [[Nate Herk]] credits as the inspiration for Level 2 — the layer that turns a folder of notes into something an AI can navigate.

## Key facts

- Founding member of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla; widely followed for practical LLM writing and teaching.
- The [[LLM Wiki]] concept: write documentation *for a model to read*, with an index and interconnected pages, so it can load only what it needs instead of the whole corpus.
