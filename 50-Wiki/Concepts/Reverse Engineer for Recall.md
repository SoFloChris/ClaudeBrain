# Reverse Engineer for Recall

Design the file architecture around **how you'll ask for things later**, not around how they arrive. [[Nate Herk]]'s framing: if you know how you need to access the data, you can build the hoop for the ball to go through.

The practical move: before filing anything, ask *"what question would retrieve this?"* — then put it where that question would look.

## Why it matters to me

It explains the whole shape of this vault:

- Notes are named for the thing you'd search (`Nate Herk`, not `video-notes-2026-07`).
- Entities get their own notes because I ask questions *about people and tools*, not about the meetings they were mentioned in.
- Relationships are typed because "who works where" is a question I'll ask; plain links can't answer it.
- Each level of the [[Second Brain Levels]] model exists to answer one question shape — exact word (L1), whole topic (L2), different words than I wrote (L3), relationship chain (L4).

It's also the argument for writing notes *proactively*: the moment I ask something the vault can't answer is proof of a question shape it should have covered. Capture the answer then, and the second time I ask, the hoop is there.
